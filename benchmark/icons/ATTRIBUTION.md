# Logos

Provider and model logos from [LobeHub Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons)
(`@lobehub/icons-static-svg` 1.45.0, MIT License). `typesafe.svg` is the TypeSafe AI
mark already used by the live table. Logos are trademarks of their owners and are
shown only to identify each model.

Add one with `python -m musbench.leaderboard icon <slug>` (slugs as listed on
https://lobehub.com/icons).
